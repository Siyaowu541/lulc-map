How are the 40 papers mapped?

The 40 uploaded PDFs were matched to 40 sheet records. Study-area and experimental-data descriptions were checked in each PDF. The original sheet has not been changed; findings are recorded here.

This is a literature index, not a reference-data footprint map.

Dashed boxes are schematic locators or reported coordinate bounds. Dots locate a city or region. Places inside a box may have no reference labels. Country shading means related papers exist; it does not mean the whole country has data. Exact point-level availability requires actual sample coordinates or image footprints.

Findings to keep in mind

  • MM-OVSeg: Pohang, South Korea, and Xi’an, China, are included in the area audit.
  • SynRS3D: virtual training cities are not shown as real ground-reference coverage.
  • OpenEarthMap, DynamicEarthNet and the OpenEarthMap challenge subset: actual regional coordinates have not yet been imported. These papers remain in the directory.
  • Five studies use image-level scene labels, such as EuroSAT or UCMerced. These are marked separately from pixel labels.
  • The Southern China Superpixel U-Net PDF identifies a 2026 volume; the source sheet says 2025. The difference is noted.
  • Two GLC_FCS30D papers concern the same product. The paper count is not the number of independent datasets.

Can this show water, forest and cropland?

Not from paper metadata alone. That needs the actual classified raster or labelled samples and their class legend. A downloadable file or a suitable online map service could supply the layer. Current colours show data access, not land-cover classes.

Access status is carried over from collected records; all downloads have not been retested. “Unverified / on request” does not mean the data are definitely private. Paper and data-source links are in each card. This atlas is a reviewed snapshot and does not automatically sync with the sheet.
