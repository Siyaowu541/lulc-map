How coverage is interpreted

Years describe the mapped results or reference imagery, not publication dates. Unreported years and synthetic scenes appear only under All years. Mixed-year composites are excluded from a single-year view unless you enable them; they are not annual maps.

FLAIR footprints are reconstructed from the official aerial-patch centroids and image size, restricted to the 50 domains in the reviewed paper. The GeoMultiTaskNet subset uses its listed 13 domains. Each footprint is filtered by its own acquisition year. MOLCA outlines are published tile extents, not masks of valid agreement pixels. Administrative boundaries locate study areas and are not sample masks. Dots locate studies whose exact footprints are unavailable.

Some globally distributed reference collections have no plotted coordinates; see the paper list. GLC_FCS30D has two publications describing one product. Access status comes from the data tracker and is not a new verification of every download link.

Sources: IGN FLAIR · MOLCA · geoBoundaries (CHN ADM1: Public Domain; CHN ADM2: PDDL; IND ADM2: ODbL) · Natural Earth. Modified and simplified for this geographic index. Administrative boundaries do not express a position on disputed sovereignty.
