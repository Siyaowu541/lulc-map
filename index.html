How coverage is interpreted

The selected year is the year represented by a paper’s land-cover results or reference imagery, not its publication year. For example, a paper published in 2025 with annual maps for 2000–2020 appears when 2002 is selected. Separate observations in 2004, 2014 and 2024 appear only in those three years; gaps are not filled. Later publications remain eligible when they describe the selected year. Unreported years and synthetic scenes appear only under All years. Mixed-year composites are excluded from a single-year view unless you enable them; they are not annual maps.

Areas represent the geographic scope of the reviewed papers, not data-download availability or a count of publications. Solid green shapes use image bounds, published tile outlines or a named regional boundary. Orange dashed shapes use administrative context, an alternative regional definition, or an approximate outline digitised from a paper figure. A city outline can be larger than the imagery actually analysed.

All 40 papers have a study-area review. Some use scattered benchmark chips without recoverable georeferenced boundaries. They remain in the list with an explanation; no arbitrary circles or world-wide filled polygons are invented. Globally distributed samples are different from global mapping products. GLC_FCS30D has two publications describing one product. Download the paper-by-paper boundary audit.
